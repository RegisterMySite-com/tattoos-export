      Pricing

      Pricing and deposits

      Every quote is confirmed in writing before you pay a deposit.

      Deposits

      A non-refundable deposit of $100 to $300 holds your appointment and comes off the final price. Deposits are paid after your consult, never before we reply.

      Cancelling or rescheduling

      Reschedule once with 48 hours notice and your deposit carries over. Cancellations or no-shows with less than 48 hours notice forfeit the deposit.
